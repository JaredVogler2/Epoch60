# Phase 16 — Meaningful long-term progression, discovery and replay desire without dark patterns

One more EPOCH should follow from curiosity and strategy, not artificial compulsive streak mechanics. Reward discovery of interactions, alternative endings, experiments across seeds and tangible long-term changes to a shared landscape. The player must never grind to unlock basic competence or wait for a countdown between sessions.

## Main run vs persistent meta

The core Chronicle is one evolving world across 8–12 generations with an attainable capstone and a clear history of causes. Independent expedition/daily are alternate challenge instances. Persistent Meta Codex collects discoveries and alternative starting drafts, but **should not provide unconditional permanent power buffs** that trivialize the early run. Keep local versioned persistence; no accounts necessary.

## Chronicle / archive book

Create interactive "Atlas of Ages" book:
- world timeline from first foundation to current age, with before/after miniatures/screenshots if captured real-time or safe scene snapshots;
- one entry per era, showing population, primary disaster, best invention, surviving monument, ecology and cause of collapse;
- map overlay of historical layers: ruins, reclaimed forests, mines, old rivercourse and centuries of construction, with selectable site and the exact originating epoch;
- discovery codex for building synergies, soil stewardship, flood engineering, archaeology finds and cultural rules;
- biographies/short vignette of notable fictional civic actors or institutions generated from actual game events, not a fabricated dialogue API.

Player must be able to press a ruin and learn its age, original purpose, why it failed, and what salvaging/rebuilding it will do **now**.

## Achievements and unlock design

At least 16 attainable achievements; examples:
- Build first archive, survive first flood with engineering, preserve every farm, restore depleted forest, uncover grandparent-era library, rebuild on old aqueduct, make three distinct age-specific technologies, win with no mining, win via regeneration, win via monumental engineering, keep a living cultural site through 5 generations, escape cycle, win while population collapsed in one age, perfect early age legacy, launch daily seed and achieve best personally recorded.
- Each triggers from real state; test with fixtures/verified traces. Never display phantom badges.
- Unlocks expand *variety*: alternate starting claim/terrain pack, blueprint choice, optional building skin with real art, challenge mutator, informative history lens. No paywall, no daily streak cost or idle wait.

## Score and feedback

Per-epoch score includes prosperity and survival; **multi-epoch legacy score** weighs sustainable land, cultural/technical knowledge, resilient infrastructure, population and historical achievements. Distinguish long-horizon success from short-term resource abuse. Show clear 3–5 item breakdown, no falsely precise ratings. Challenge mode best score compares only identical seed/rules/assistance/quality-independent engine versions. Avoid score-farming via build/demolish/salvage loops and pause.

Successor reveal should be the emotional reward: dramatic artful time travel plus concise "because you built X in era 2, Y survived" and a new route option. A failed civilization still yields artifact discovery or learned cause, providing a specific new strategy rather than meaningless consolation currency.

## Replay modes

- Replay identical seed from era 1 with reset history.
- New procedural world with new resources/terrain/hazards.
- Daily fixed UTC seed, locally stored score no claimed global rank.
- Branchable save at new-epoch boundary for unranked experiments, clearly mark diverged lineage and avoid ranked exploits.
- Short optional challenges e.g. 3 epochs to build floodproof bridge, preserve living forest, excavate mythic observatory.
- Continue Endless after capstone if desired.

## Acceptance

A player who wins/loses has one-tap Retry/New World and can identify at least two meaningful alternatives for a next run; codex progress persists, old branches don't corrupt new runs, achievements are reachable, and personal best is comparable only on matched parameters. A new game should not start with a large mandatory tutorial again; skip available. Ensure no coercive notification/streak/purchase UX. Commit fixtures and tested UI flows.
