## What is and is not claimed

- Targets (60 fps on a midrange laptop, 30 fps on a midrange iPhone) are **unproven**: no real-device measurement exists. The adaptive
  governor (`src/render/governor.ts`, unit-tested) is the mitigation: it lowers the pixel ratio first, then sheds post-processing and
  finally the birds and the decorative valley beyond the board. It never removes geometry the player needs or any forecast information.
- Draw-call and triangle counts are real counts from `renderer.info` for one whole frame, including the shadow pass and, on the high tier,
  every post-processing pass.
- For comparison, before session 4's visual overhaul the same scene cost 118 draw calls and 344k / 440k / 549k triangles (low / medium /
  high). The overhaul cut draw calls (one instanced smoke system instead of per-chimney meshes) and added triangles, mostly the
  smooth-shaded trees and the valley beyond the board; the latter uses far-detail trees (56–160 triangles instead of 240–344).
- Session 5 gave the villagers headwear and a tool by job (seven instanced meshes of at most 40 each in place of one): 5 more draw calls and about 6k
  more triangles than the session-4 record (81 / 84 draw calls). Hats and tools skip the shadow pass, where they would be invisible.
- Session 6's history lens is not in this scene: it is drawn only between ages and, on request, while planning an inherited age. It costs
  one draw call per catastrophe so far, one for eroded ground and one per age and state (standing or ruin) of the buildings, at most 8 in a
  three-age lineage, and nothing while hidden. The probe does not measure it.
- Session 6's then-and-now pictures are not in this scene either: twice per age the game draws one extra frame of the valley into a
  480×270 corner of the canvas (from a fixed viewpoint, overlays hidden) and reads it back, then redraws the normal view. The probe does not
  measure it.
- Session 7's omens (Phase 19) are not in this scene either: they draw only in an age's last 30 s, and then only the age's
  own hazard: for a fire one instanced smoke column (at most 40 puffs) and one point cloud of 160 embers, for a flood 900
  rain streaks in one line draw, for a quake one instanced mesh of at most 48 dust puffs; every one skips the shadow pass,
  and the CPU work is one loop over those instances per frame. The low tier draws 60% of the embers and rain. The
  building surface pass adds no draw call (the same material with cloud shadows, contact shading and a roof finish in its
  shader); the outskirts gain one instanced mesh of bushes (on about a quarter of the open cells within 12 of the board). Unmeasured on a real GPU (K-009).
- Session 7's later additions, measured with the same probe after them (showcase scene, not re-recorded in the table
  above): 87 / 87 / 90 draw calls (one more: the outskirts' bushes) and 723,030 / 976,474 / 1,210,013 triangles at low /
  medium / high (+12k, +22k, +35k, about 2–3%), mostly the Age of Iron's two-storey houses and their masonry, and the
  bushes.
